### Objective for the Week

The objective of Week 1 was to understand the telemedicine problem, research existing solutions and technologies, identify the major system requirements, and contribute to the preparation of the project proposal.

### Day 1 — Problem Understanding

Analysed the need for a digital platform connecting patients and doctors for remote consultations.
- Identified the key challenges in appointment management and online doctor patient communication.

### Day 2 — Existing Systems & Research

Studied existing telemedicine platforms and their major features.
- Analysed common workflows including doctor discovery, appointment booking, consultation, and communication.

### Day 3 — Technology Research

Researched the proposed technology stack including Next.js, Node.js, Express, MongoDB, Firebase, Socket.io, WebRTC, and Gemini API.
- Evaluated their suitability for the planned platform.

### Day 4 — Feature Planning

Identified the major features of the platform including patient and doctor portals, appointments, chat, video consultation, notifications, and AI assisted symptom intake.
- Discussed the initial division of these features among team members.

### Day 5 — Technical Planning

Worked on the initial technical structure of the platform.
- Defined the major frontend, backend, database, real time communication, and AI components.

### Day 6 — Proposal Finalisation

Reviewed and refined the project requirements, scope, and technical approach.
- Contributed to finalising the project proposal with the team.

### Week 1 Deliverables

- Finalised the project problem and objective.
- Researched existing telemedicine solutions and technologies.
- Identified major system features and requirements.
- Defined the initial technical stack and system components.
- Prepared and refined the project proposal.

### Individual Contribution

My primary contribution was researching and planning the **real time communication and AI components** of the platform. I focused on Socket.io based chat, WebRTC video consultation, Gemini AI integration, real time notifications, and the initial AI safety requirements.

### Note

The features discussed during Week 1 were part of the **proposed project scope and technical planning** and were not implemented during this week.
