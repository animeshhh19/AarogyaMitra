### Objective for the Week

The objective of Week 2 was to convert the requirements identified in Week 1 into a structured system architecture and define the technical workflow of the major platform components.

### Day 1 — System Architecture

Designed the high level architecture connecting the frontend, backend, database, authentication, real time services, and AI assistant.
- Defined the basic flow of data between the major components.

### Day 2 — Database & API Planning

Identified the major database entities such as users, appointments, consultations, and messages.
- Planned the initial REST API structure required by the frontend.

### Day 3 — Real Time Layer Planning

Designed the Socket.io architecture for consultation specific communication.
- Defined the basic flow for chat, consultation events, and real time notifications.

### Day 4 — Video Consultation Planning

Researched and planned the WebRTC based video consultation workflow.
- Defined the role of Socket.io signalling and basic connection and reconnection handling.

### Day 5 — AI Assistant Planning

Designed the Gemini based pre consultation symptom intake workflow.
- Planned how patient responses could be converted into a structured summary for the doctor.

### Day 6 — Technical Review

Reviewed the complete architecture and integration requirements with the team.
- Refined the technical scope, safety requirements, and development roadmap.

### Week 2 Deliverables

- Defined the overall system architecture.
- Planned the database and REST API structure.
- Designed the Socket.io real time communication layer.
- Planned the WebRTC video consultation workflow.
- Defined the Gemini AI symptom intake workflow.
- Established the initial AI safety and integration requirements.

### Individual Contribution

My primary contribution was the design of the **real time and AI portions** of the system. I focused on the Socket.io consultation room architecture, WebRTC communication flow, real time notifications, Gemini API integration, symptom intake workflow, and initial AI safety requirements.

### Note

The architecture and workflows developed during Week 2 represent the **technical design and implementation plan**. Actual implementation was scheduled for the subsequent development weeks.
