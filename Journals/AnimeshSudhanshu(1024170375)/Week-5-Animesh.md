Objective for the Week

The objective of Week 5 was to build the remaining portal screens: the admin portal for doctor approval and analytics, the AI pre-consultation assistant chat screen, and the medical records pages.

Day 1 — Admin Portal Base

Built the admin portal layout and a doctor approval screen that lists pending doctors with their specialty, degrees and fee, and lets the admin approve or reject each one.

Added a patient accounts screen for the admin, and made sure the admin portal is reachable only by users with the admin role.

Day 2 — Doctor Onboarding Status

Added an approval status banner on the doctor dashboard, for example showing a newly signed-up doctor that their profile is pending approval and is not yet visible to patients.

Updated the doctor profile form to cover specialty, price, degrees and schedule, so a doctor can complete everything the admin needs before approving.

Day 3 — AI Assistant Chat Screen

Built the AI pre-consultation assistant screen, with streamed replies so the patient sees the answer appear as it is generated.

Added a clear notice on the screen that the assistant only helps prepare context for the doctor and does not diagnose or prescribe, and showed the remaining hourly quota to the patient.

Day 4 — Emergency Response and PDF Upload

Designed the display for the assistant's emergency response, so when the safety layer flags a symptom the patient sees a prominent seek-immediate-care message instead of normal advice.

Added the option to attach a PDF, for example a previous report, in the assistant chat and handled upload errors in the UI.

Day 5 — Medical Records and Analytics Views

Built the medical records pages, so a patient can view and add records and a doctor can view the records of their own patients.

Built the admin analytics view showing bookings, booking completion rate and time-to-connect from the backend's analytics endpoints.

Day 6 — Integration and Review

Did a joint test with the team across the admin approval flow, the AI assistant including an emergency symptom, the records pages and the analytics view, using accounts for a patient, a doctor and an admin.

Reviewed responsiveness on smaller screens and fixed layout issues in the admin tables and the assistant chat, and logged the remaining work: final polish and fixes from the pilot.

Week 5 Deliverables

Admin portal with doctor approval, patient accounts and analytics views.

Doctor approval status banner and complete doctor profile form.

AI assistant chat screen with streaming replies, quota display and emergency response.

PDF upload in the assistant chat.

Medical records pages for patients and doctors.
